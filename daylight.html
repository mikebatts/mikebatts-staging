Solar is not one transaction. It is a chain of promises.

A homeowner meets a seller, an underwriter, an installer, a utility, and a long-term energy provider. Every handoff creates another place for context to disappear. Daylight gives everyone one project, one current state, and one next step.

The iPhone app turns a technical energy system into a daily relationship. Homeowners can understand what their home is doing, see where energy went, and manage payments without learning the machinery behind it.
