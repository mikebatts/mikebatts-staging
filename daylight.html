One project

One home. Many hands. One shared state.

Going solar is not buying a gadget. It is construction on a roof, permits from the town, permission from the utility, financing, and money moving between several companies for years. A homeowner should feel one calm project. Underneath, a group of companies has to move in step and never lose the thread.

Shared project state 1 of 4 verified

Nobody works blind, and nothing advances until it is genuinely done. Because every party writes to one shared project state instead of their own copy, a signature, an approval, and a permit can never quietly disagree. That is the whole problem this case study is about.

My role

I take complex systems and make them feel simple.

As founding product engineer and manager, I organized a small team into cross-functional pods and stayed hands on across prototypes, surface design, backend architecture, and production code. I moved between Figma and the shipping build daily, and set the review discipline that kept quality the default while we moved fast.

Daylight is the clearest proof of what I do best: taking a genuinely complex, regulated, multi-company system and shipping the surfaces that make it feel simple and trustworthy to everyone inside it. Ray is where that same instinct meets AI, grounded, guarded, and honest about what it does not know.
