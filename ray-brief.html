Ray

Daylight

Product direction, design & implementation

An assistant that fits around the work.

Answers to project questions, right where people need them.

Context

Daylight’s partner portal helps teams coordinate solar installations. Project Notes revealed a recurring problem: people were asking questions whose answers already existed in project records or the knowledge base.

A teammate had added a basic OpenAI chat to the project-management kanban. I expanded it across the portal and turned it into a feature, leading the product direction, design and implementation.

Fit around the task

Ray opens as a side drawer, full screen or a separate mini window. The conversation and unsent draft carry across views and portal navigation. People can keep a project in sight without starting over.

A CSV was only half the answer

We released Ray to Daylight Ops internally first. Interviews and transcripts surfaced requests for CSVs, but the underlying need was often a working list people could return to.

I added sheets that save the query and run it again when opened. Each viewer sees only what their permissions allow. CSV export stays available.

What I took away

The useful question wasn’t just “can Ray answer this?” It was “what does someone need to do next?” Retrieval checks were a start, not proof that the final answer was right.
