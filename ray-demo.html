Ask about Orchard House.

A fictional solar project with sample records. Ray answers three kinds of question here, with scripted responses.

About this demo

Responses are scripted for three sample tasks and come from fixtures in this page, not from a live model. The project, its records, the people and every reference number are fictional.

Nothing you type is sent anywhere. The conversation stays in this browser tab until you close it or choose Start over. Filing a document, posting a reply and asking for a person are simulated locally and say so.

Unsupported questions get a short explanation and the three examples above.
