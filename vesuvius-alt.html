The challenge, in plain English

Read the page.
Never touch the scroll.

Mount Vesuvius buried Herculaneum and its private library. Heat turned hundreds of papyrus scrolls into fragile, tightly wound blocks of carbon. Together, they form the only intact library to survive from the ancient world.

Unroll one and it can crumble. The Vesuvius Challenge asks us to read the writing digitally: scan the sealed object, reconstruct a page inside it, and detect ink without ever opening the scroll.
